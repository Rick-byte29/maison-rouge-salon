GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
take the chair.

Demo FAQ copy. Replace with branch policy before a real launch.

Do I need a consultation for colour?+

For major colour changes, correction, balayage or first-time visits, a short consultation is recommended so timing and pricing can be planned accurately.

How long does keratin or smoothening take?+

Timing depends on length, density and treatment choice. Plan roughly 2–4 hours for most smoothing services.

Can I book bridal hair trials?+

Yes. A trial helps align hairstyle, outfit, jewellery, veil/dupatta placement and event timing before the wedding day.

Do you take walk-ins?+

Walk-ins can be accommodated when chairs are free, but appointments are recommended for colour, treatments and occasion styling.

Why can the final price vary?+

Hair length, density, product usage, previous chemical history and desired result can change service time and material requirements.
